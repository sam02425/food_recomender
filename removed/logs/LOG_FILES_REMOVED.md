## Log Content
These logs contained:
- Real-time experiment execution logs
- Participant interaction tracking
- System performance metrics
- Error and debug information
- Trial completion status

## Purpose of Removal
Log files were removed because:
1. They were generated by autonomous systems during testing
2. They contained operational data not needed for research publication
3. Essential research data was extracted and preserved in the data files
4. They would clutter the research repository

## Data Preservation
The important experimental data from these logs has been:
- Extracted and summarized in `removed/human_experiments_data/`
- Included in the research results documentation
- Preserved in aggregate form for statistical analysis
